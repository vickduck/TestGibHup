Everything you need to automate

From simple tasks to complex multi-branch workflows, FlowForge has the tools to get it done.

🖱️

Visual Drag-and-Drop Builder

Design workflows visually on an infinite canvas. Connect nodes, branch logic, and see your automation come to life in real time.

🧩

400+ Pre-built Integrations

Connect to Slack, GitHub, Stripe, Salesforce, Google Sheets, and hundreds more. Add custom APIs in minutes.

🤖

AI-Powered Nodes

Summarise, classify, extract, and generate with built-in AI nodes powered by GPT-4o, Claude, and Gemini.

Self-Host or Cloud

Run on our managed cloud or self-host on your own infrastructure. Your data, your rules. SOC 2 Type II compliant.

→ How it works

Up and running in minutes

No complex setup. No steep learning curve. Just connect, build, and ship.

Connect your apps

Authenticate once and FlowForge remembers your credentials securely. OAuth, API keys, and tokens all supported.

Build your workflow

Drag nodes onto the canvas, wire them together, and configure each step. Use our templates to get started fast.

Test & iterate

Run your workflow with real data, inspect every step's output, and debug visually. No more console.log debugging.

Deploy & monitor

Activate your workflow with one click. Monitor execution logs, set up error alerts, and scale automatically.
